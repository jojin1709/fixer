How it develops

  1. 01

    Load

    Drop in any number of JPG, PNG or WebP photos. They stay on your machine — nothing is sent anywhere.

  2. 02

    Set the exposure

    Pick a quality level and, optionally, a max width. The browser's own canvas does the re-encoding.

  3. 03

    Pull the prints

    Download each photo, grab the whole batch as a .zip, or stack every frame into a single PDF.

Fixer processes everything locally in your browser. No files are uploaded.
