frequently asked questions

Common Questions

How does Fixer compress images without uploading?

Fixer uses the browser's native HTML5 Canvas 2D API to decode, resize, and re-encode photos directly inside your computer or phone RAM. No files are ever transmitted over a network socket.

Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG or WebP?

Yes. Fixer automatically decodes iOS .heic and .heif camera files in-browser via WebAssembly, allowing instant conversion into high-quality JPEG, WebP, AVIF, or PNG.

Does Fixer remove private location and camera metadata?

Yes. When images are processed on our HTML5 Canvas sandbox, all embedded GPS location coordinates, camera models, and private timestamps are automatically stripped for total privacy.

How do I compile multiple photos into a single PDF?

Drop your photos into Fixer, click Develop all, and then click Stack into PDF. You can choose between 1 photo per page or 2×2 / 3×3 contact sheets with custom margins.
